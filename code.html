Discrete Variable repeat

Binomial Distribution

$P(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1-p)^{n-k}$

Models the total number of successes in $n$ strictly independent trials with constant success probability $p$.

Key Assumptions:
  • Fixed number of trials $n$
  • Dichotomous outcomes (success / failure)
  • Independence between individual draws
Real-world: A/B Tests, Quality inspection arrow_forward
Counting Process call_received

Poisson Distribution

$P(X = k) = \frac{\lambda^k e^{-\lambda}}{k!}$

Expresses the probability of a given count of events occurring in a fixed interval of time or space at an invariant average rate $\lambda$.

Poisson Limit Theorem:

As $n \to \infty$ and $p \to 0$ with $\lambda = np$ holding constant, the Binomial converges to Poisson.

Real-world: Packet queues, Call centers arrow_forward
Continuous Baseline all_inclusive

Normal (Gaussian)

$Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$

Governed by the Central Limit Theorem: sums of independent identically distributed variables approach normality regardless of parent distribution.

Empirical Rule (68-95-99.7):
  • $68.27\%$ lies in $[\mu \pm \sigma]$
  • $95.45\%$ lies in $[\mu \pm 2\sigma]$
  • $99.73\%$ lies in $[\mu \pm 3\sigma]$
Real-world: Measurement error, Biometrics arrow_forward
Survival / Waiting hourglass_top

Exponential Family

$P(X > s + t \mid X > s) = P(X > t)$

Models waiting times between continuous Poisson events. The unique continuous distribution with the memoryless property.

Survival Function:

$S(t) = P(X > t) = e^{-\lambda t}$. Constant instantaneous hazard rate $\lambda$.

Real-world: Component MTBF, Decay arrow_forward

Cloud Server Outage Risk

A datacenter experiences an average of $\lambda = 3.2$ critical outages per month. What is the probability of having 5 or more failures next month?

Step-by-step resolution:

$P(X \ge 5) = 1 - \sum_{k=0}^4 \frac{3.2^k e^{-3.2}}{k!}$

$P(X < 5) = e^{-3.2}\left(1 + 3.2 + \frac{3.2^2}{2} + \frac{3.2^3}{6} + \frac{3.2^4}{24}\right) \approx 0.7806$

Result: $P(X \ge 5)$ 21.94%
Growth & CRO Analytics shopping_cart

E-Commerce Checkout Funnel

With a benchmark conversion rate of $p = 8\%$ and a cohort sample of $n = 50$ visitors, what is the exact probability of securing at least 6 orders?

Step-by-step resolution:

$\mathbb{E}[X] = n \cdot p = 50 \cdot 0.08 = 4.0$

$P(X \ge 6) = 1 - \sum_{k=0}^5 \binom{50}{k} 0.08^k (0.92)^{50-k} \approx 0.209$

Result: $P(X \ge 6)$ 20.92%

Shaft Diameter Tolerance

Machined shafts have nominal $\mu = 50.00\text{ mm}$ and $\sigma = 0.05\text{ mm}$. Specification limits are $[49.90, 50.10]\text{ mm}$. What is the defect rate?

Step-by-step resolution:

$Z_1 = \frac{49.90 - 50}{0.05} = -2.0, \quad Z_2 = \frac{50.10 - 50}{0.05} = +2.0$

$P(\text{In Spec}) = \Phi(2.0) - \Phi(-2.0) = 0.9545$
